Affordability for owner-occupiers

Banco de España series back to 1987. The price-to-income ratio counts how many years of gross household income an average home costs. Mortgage effort is the share of a year's income that goes on payments for a typical new mortgage. Comparing the two separates the price level from the cost of credit.

Who's buying: local vs foreign demand

Notarised home purchases split by buyer: Spanish nationals, foreigners living in Spain, and foreigners buying from abroad (non-residents). Non-residents are the closest proxy for footloose international money. Source: Ministry of Housing, quarterly by province since 2007, shown as rolling four-quarter totals.

If foreign money disappears: where sales are exposed

Set a drop in foreign purchases. The table shows the direct hit to each province's sales over a year, before any knock-on effect on prices or local buyers. It covers the 15 provinces most reliant on foreign buyers.
